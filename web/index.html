HOW IT WORKS

A little help goes a long way

Share your trip

Older adults, women traveling alone, and family members can list the route, travel date, language, and kind of help needed.

Find a fellow traveler

Volunteers can offer help on a flight they’re already taking. Look for the same airports and travel date, then confirm the flight and any connections.

Agree on the details

Meet in a public area at the airport. Ask your airline directly for wheelchair, medical, or other formal assistance. Keep passport and booking details private.
